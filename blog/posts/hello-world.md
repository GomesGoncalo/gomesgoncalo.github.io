# Hello, World

> "First, solve the problem. Then, write the code." — John Johnson

This is the first post on my blog. Consider it a placeholder that grew arms and legs.

## Why a blog?

I've been meaning to write more for a while. Notes from deep dives, things I wish had existed as a reference when I was learning something, opinions on system design — that kind of thing.

Writing is also just useful. It forces you to find the gaps in your own understanding. If you can't explain it clearly, you probably don't understand it clearly.

## What to expect

Mostly technical, mostly systems-adjacent:

- **Systems programming** — networking stacks, embedded middleware, memory models
- **Architecture** — how to structure software that doesn't collapse under its own weight
- **Open source** — lessons from contributing to projects I use daily
- **Miscellaneous** — whatever I'm currently reading or obsessing over

The posting cadence will be irregular. Quality over quantity.
